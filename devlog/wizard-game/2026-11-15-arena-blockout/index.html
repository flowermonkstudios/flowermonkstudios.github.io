What worked

Projectile spells feel good once there’s a 3-frame hitstop on impact.

What didn’t

Enemies clip through pillar corners. Probably need proper collision shapes instead of boxes.

A bit of code

func cast(spell: Spell) -> void:
    var p := spell.projectile.instantiate()
    p.direction = aim_vector()
    get_parent().add_child(p)
